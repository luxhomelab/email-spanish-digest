Supreme Court halts ley de nietos voting rights

The Supreme Court suspended voting rights for people who obtained Spanish nationality through the Democratic Memory Law's ley de nietos provision, citing electoral transparency after a sharp rise in the overseas census. The measure affects new CERA registrations and those already registered, though it makes an exception for applicants with documented family ties to exiles. The CERA shows 408,262 more registered voters as of July 1, 2026, than in the 2023 general election census. The court acted after appeals by Iustitia Europa and Vox.

2 🏠 Property

ECB rate hike adds 55-75 euros monthly to variable mortgages

The ECB raised interest rates by 25 basis points to 2.5%, the highest since April 2025, and borrowers with variable-rate mortgages will pay roughly €55 to €75 more a month. ASUFIN expects Euribor to close September at 3.2%, making the average applied rate about 4.20% with a typical 1% spread, compared with a 2.172% reference a year earlier. A €100,000 mortgage over 25 years would rise from €483.21 to €538.94 monthly, or €668.84 more a year. The unanimous decision targets inflation driven by Middle East energy prices.

3 💰 Economy

Brent oil tops $105 as European gas hits 2022 highs

Brent crude surpassed $105 a barrel on Thursday, and European gas reached its highest level since December 2022, adding to inflation pressures the same day the ECB raised rates. Brent rose by up to 4% and has climbed more than 70% this year, while US benchmark WTI passed $100. TTF European gas rose another 2.5% to exceed €82 per megawatt hour, a rise of more than 155% since the start of the war in Iran. Higher energy prices push up household costs across Spain.

4 📋 Visa

Congress approves Spanish nationality law for Sahrawis

Congress approved a bill granting Spanish nationality to Sahrawis born when Western Sahara was under Spanish administration and to their descendants. The measure passed with support from government parties and their usual allies; PP and UPN abstained, and Vox cast the only vote against. It creates a new route to citizenship for this group. The law feeds into the wider debate on nationality and migration rules in Spain.

5 👥 Work

Ford offers Almussafes staff pay rises for Geely flexibility

Ford presented its final offer to the Almussafes (Valencia) workforce through 2030, combining pay increases above real CPI with up to eight mandatory production Saturdays a year. For 2027, the increase equals real CPI plus 1.5 percentage points, with a guaranteed minimum of 3% and a maximum of 5%. The offer raises the extraordinary bonus and adds new incentives. It follows Ford's deal with China's Geely to bring new models to the plant and secure its industrial future.

8 🏠 Property

Housing aid: up to 40 million euros for affordable rent

The Ministry of Housing will grant direct subsidies totaling up to €40.41 million for rehousing, affordable or social rental housing, and protected housing under a Royal Decree published in the BOE. Barcelona City Council receives up to €15,777,360 to build affordable or social rental housing, and up to €250,000 to organize the Housing Summit 2026. The Community of Madrid gets up to €8,375,000 to rehouse families in Cañada Real and fund habitability works. The subsidies bypass a public call for applications for reasons of public, social and economic interest.

9 👥 Work

Delayed retirement lump sum covers half of pension gain

A Fedea study finds workers who delay retirement and take the lump-sum payment receive about half of what they would get with a lifetime pension increase. Researchers analyzed 280 profiles combining sex, age, years of delay and pension level. In none of them was there actuarial equivalence; the lifetime increase always won. For the average retiree, every €51.20 from the lump sum could have been €100 with the lifetime supplement.

10 🏛️ Politics

Crime among under-14s soars nearly 75%, prosecutors warn

The Fiscalía's 2025 report says crime by children under 14, who are not criminally liable, rose 74.21%. Cases archived because those investigated were under 14 totaled 7,749, up from 4,448 a year earlier. Gender violence cases among minors rose 15.20% to 1,023, the highest in five years, and crimes against sexual freedom rose 35.82%. The Fiscalía links the trend to social media abuse and disinformation targeting minors.

12 💰 Economy

Uber's driverless taxis in Madrid get green light

Uber, WeRide and Avomo received Spain's first authorization for autonomous passenger robotaxis from the DGT and the Community of Madrid, with tests planned for late 2026. The permit allows autonomous passenger vehicles in delimited areas of public roads. Initial work includes mapping operating zones, validating routes and testing vehicle operability. A new mobility option in Madrid could eventually change transport costs and availability.
