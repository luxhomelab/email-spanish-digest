1 💰 Economy

All Spanish workers face new payroll deduction from 2027

The MEI pension mechanism will rise to 1% as of January 1, 2027. Workers will contribute 0.17% of their base salary, up from 0.15% in 2026, while employers will pay 0.83%. The increase will hit all workers, with no salary-level exceptions; for example, an employee earning €25,000 gross pays about €37.50 this year.

2 🏠 Property

Community approval now required for temporary rentals

Spain's Horizontal Property Law, which has been in force since April 2025, now requires community approval for temporary or non-residential rentals. Approval requires a three-fifths majority of both owners and ownership shares—six out of ten owners. Communities of owners can reject the rental use, impose a special fee, or raise common expenses by up to 20%.

3 💰 Economy

ECB hike intensifies battle for Spanish savers

After the European Central Bank raised interest rates for the second time this year, banks are competing harder for deposits. Neobanks such as ING, MyInvestor and Trade Republic now offer 3% on their main products, while large banks focus on investment funds, pensions and payroll accounts. Variable-rate mortgages will become more expensive.

4 🏥 Health

Doctors call indefinite national strike starting October 28

Medical unions have called an indefinite national strike starting October 28 to reject the Estatuto Marco bill and demand a separate statute for doctors. The unions CESM, SMA, Metges de Catalunya, AMYTS, SME and O'MEGA announced the action 10 days after the Council of Ministers approved the bill. Patients may face delays in appointments and hospital care.

Fuel stations demand urgent return of discounts

7 🏠 Property

Spain to subsidize 2,425 affordable rental homes

The government plans to issue a decree granting up to €201.7 million to private operators to build 2,425 affordable rental homes on public land under the Casa 47 program. The state will provide land and funding for companies to build and manage the rentals. The average subsidy is about €83,000 per home across 29 projects.

10 🏛️ Politics

Ayuso to challenge census expansion for 'Ley de Nietos' voters

Madrid's president, Isabel Díaz Ayuso, will file objections with the Junta Electoral Central to exclude voters with 'greater ties' from the CERA census. She wants the expansion to include only voters abroad whose last residence was Madrid. The move follows the Supreme Court's suspension of the census for those who obtained nationality under the Ley de Nietos.

A draft decree requiring new renewable plants to supply 80% of data centers' electricity is drawing criticism from renewable and industrial sectors. 13 📅 Events

New zinc-air battery design boosts power by 80%

Researchers at ICMAB-CSIC have developed a new architecture for zinc-air batteries that increases available power by up to 80%. The key is a physical reconfiguration of the internal cell space, with no change to the chemical composition. The design avoids scarce materials and uses abundant zinc and atmospheric oxygen.
