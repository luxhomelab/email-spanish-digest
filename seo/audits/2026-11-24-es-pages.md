# SEO audit: Spanish pages (/es/), 2026-11-24

Scope: every page under `dist/es/` after `py -3 build.py --out dist` (242 pages: home, 197 digests, 20 archive pages, 10 categories, 6 articles plus the list, subscribe, legal and the 3 noindex form-flow pages), plus the English twins as far as hreflang, og:locale and the sitemap go. Reference pair: `/subscribe` and `/es/subscribe`.

The new invariants are in `test_es_seo.py`, which runs a full build. It checks html lang, og:locale on every /es/ page, title and description bounds, uniqueness, self canonical, og:url, reciprocal hreflang with the English twin, og:locale pairs on both twins, JSON-LD language and @ids, sitemap coverage with alternates, noindex pages left out, digest/archive/category lastmod matching English, and llms.txt.

## Fixes

1. `digest_context(data, lang, title_subject=True)`: `main()` passes `False` for Spanish editions whose English twin has no subject, so the meta title leads with the top story ("… | Noticias de España, 17 jun"), as the English twin does. The subject stays as the on-page subheader. `dedupe_digest_titles` now keys on the `title_subject` that was actually used.
2. `templates/base.html` emits one `og:locale` from `page_lang` (es_ES / en_GB) on every page. Pages with a twin (`alt_path`, the same value hreflang uses) also get `og:locale:alternate`. The per-template og:locale tags in `article.html`, `index.html` and `es/{subscribe,privacy,terms,cookies}.html` were removed, so no page has two.
3. `templates/archive.html` and `templates/articles.html` set `meta_title` / `meta_desc` once and emit the full OG and Twitter set from them.
4. `digest_meta_description` skips story titles that do not fit instead of stopping at the first one. When at least two stories are left over, it closes with "; y N noticias más" ("; plus N more stories"). All Spanish digest descriptions are now 100 to 157 characters. This also lengthens the English descriptions (same function).
5. `ES_STRINGS` / `CATEGORY_META_ES`: " — " became " | " in titles and ", página N" in pagination. The visa and migration category titles were shortened to stay at 70 characters or less.
6. The new Spanish list title is "Guías prácticas para vivir en España | Spanified".
7. The descriptions were tightened in `data/articles_es/*.md` frontmatter and `templates/es/privacy.html` / `terms.html`.
8. `templates/digest.html` uses `digest.date_display` in the JSON-LD description. On Spanish pages, `articleSection` is the Spanish category name (English output unchanged).
9. `"inLanguage": "es"` was added to the Spanish ItemList in `archive.html` and `category.html`.
10. `render_llms_txt` adds a "Spanish edition (España al Día)" section listing `/es/`, `/es/archive/`, `/es/articles/` and `/es/subscribe` when those twins exist. `robots.txt` needs nothing: it allows everything and points at the one sitemap, which lists all /es/ pages.

## Deferred items and checks that passed

- **#11 title length.** The 82-character cap is a site-wide rule shared with the English digests: the story lead goes first, so the truncated part is the suffix. Moving the Spanish cap to 60 would cut most story leads with an ellipsis. Revisit both languages together if Search Console shows rewritten titles. `test_es_seo.py` pins digests at 82 characters or less and every other Spanish page at 70 or less.
- **#12** is documented in the earlier session notes. The English quizzes, videos and social channels have no Spanish versions, and the copy around them says so.
- **#13** These pages are noindex, have no English-language SEO value and are not in the sitemap, so no change is needed.
- **#14** Article lastmod is max(`updated`, last git commit of the source). `data/articles_es/` is not committed yet, so it falls back to `updated`. That is correct per page. Once committed, it tracks the Spanish file's own history.
- **Passed:** `<html lang="es">` on every page. Self canonical, og:url and reciprocal en/es/x-default hreflang on all 239 indexable pages. No hreflang points at a missing page. Pagination is handled the same way as English (self canonical per page, paired hreflang). Every indexable /es/ page is in the sitemap with matching `xhtml:link` alternates, noindex pages are absent, and digest/archive/category lastmod equals English. All JSON-LD parses, carries `inLanguage: "es"` and uses /es/ @ids. Titles and descriptions are unique. There are no English UI strings outside the deliberate items. Dates use the Spanish format ("17 de junio de 2026"). English pages expose the Spanish section through the header switcher (every page), hreflang and the sitemap. No thin categories: the smallest Spanish category is migration, with 29 stories.
