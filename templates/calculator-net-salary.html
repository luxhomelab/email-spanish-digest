      From gross to net in Spain

      Two deductions take you from the gross in your contract to the money in your account. Social Security is a flat 6.50% on a permanent contract, up to a ceiling of €5,101.20 a month; above it you only pay a small solidarity fee. IRPF is progressive and split in two halves: the state scale is the same everywhere, the regional scale depends on where you live. Before the scales apply, you deduct Social Security, a €2,000 expense allowance and, on low salaries, a reduction of up to €7,302; your personal and family minimum is then taxed at 0%.

      Your employer does not know your full tax situation, so it withholds IRPF with the national AEAT algorithm. The Renta in spring settles the real figure with your region's scale and deductions. That is why many people in Madrid get money back and many in Catalonia or Valencia pay a little more.

      Sources: Orden PJC/297/2026 (Social Security bases and rates); Ley 35/2006 del IRPF, arts. 19, 20, 56–61, 68.4, 84, 93 and DA 61ª; RD 439/2007, art. 81; AEAT withholding algorithm 2026; AEAT Manual Renta 2025: regional scales and regional minimums. Regional deductions are not included. Last verified: October {{ calc_year }}. An estimate, not tax advice.
