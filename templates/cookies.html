Cookie Policy

Effective: November 6, 2026. Analytics and ad cookies load only if you say yes.

What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies (such as your browser's local storage) are small pieces of data a website keeps on your device. EU law (the ePrivacy Directive, and in Spain article 22.2 of the LSSI) lets a site use the ones it strictly needs without asking. Everything else requires your consent first.

Your choice

The first time you visit, a banner asks whether we may use analytics and marketing cookies. “Reject all” is just as easy as “Accept all”, and nothing optional loads until you choose. We remember your answer for 12 months, then ask again.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time: open cookie settings (the same link is in the footer of every page). If you withdraw, we delete the analytics and marketing cookies on our domain and reload the page.
