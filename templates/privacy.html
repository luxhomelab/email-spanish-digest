Privacy Policy

Effective: September 14, 2026. Short version: we collect almost nothing, and never sell anything.

Who we are

Spanified publishes the Spain Daily newsletter and this website. For any privacy question, write to support@spanified.com — we usually reply within a couple of days.

What we collect

Newsletter email. When you subscribe, we store your email address to send you the Spain Daily digest. Subscription and delivery are handled by our email provider Brevo (Sendinblue), which processes your address on our behalf.

Anonymous analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand which pages are read — no names, no emails, no cross-site tracking on our side.

We do not run accounts, comments, or trackers beyond that. The site is free to read with no sign-up.

Why we use it (GDPR basis)

Your email is used solely to deliver the newsletter you asked for — the legal basis is your consent, given at subscription. Analytics are covered by our legitimate interest in keeping the site useful. You can withdraw consent anytime with one click via the unsubscribe link in every email.

How long we keep it

Your email stays on the mailing list until you unsubscribe — unsubscribed addresses are removed. Anonymous analytics are retained per Google's standard retention settings.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to show, correct, or delete your data, restrict or object to its use, and take it elsewhere (portability). Just email support@spanified.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority — in Spain, the AEPD.

Sharing

We share your email only with Brevo for delivery. We never sell personal data and never share it with advertisers. If the law requires disclosure, we will comply — and tell you unless forbidden.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will note it here and, for subscribers, in the newsletter. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
