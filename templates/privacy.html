Privacy Policy

Effective: November 6, 2026. Short version: we collect almost nothing, and never sell anything.

Who we are

Spanified publishes the Spain Daily newsletter and this website. For any privacy question, write to support@spanified.com — we usually reply within a couple of days.

What we collect

Newsletter email. When you subscribe, we store your email address to send you the Spain Daily digest. Subscriptions are managed in Listmonk, the newsletter software we run, and the emails are sent through an email delivery service that processes your address on our behalf. The subscribe form is protected by Cloudflare (Turnstile bot check), which handles your submission in transit.

Analytics and ad measurement — only with your consent. If you allow it in the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics 4 to see which pages are read, and the Meta Pixel to measure sign-ups from our Facebook and Instagram ads. These set cookies and send your IP address, device and page-visit data to Google and Meta. If you say no, neither loads. Details are in our Cookie Policy.

We do not run accounts, comments, or trackers beyond that. The site is free to read with no sign-up.

Why we use it (GDPR basis)

Your email is used solely to deliver the newsletter you asked for — the legal basis is your consent, given at subscription. Analytics and ad-measurement cookies rely on your consent too (GDPR art. 6(1)(a) and article 22.2 of Spain's LSSI). You can withdraw consent anytime: for the newsletter via the unsubscribe link in every email, for cookies via cookie settings.

How long we keep it

Your email stays on the mailing list until you unsubscribe — unsubscribed addresses are removed. Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in Google Analytics (2 or 14 months); your cookie choice is remembered for 12 months.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to show, correct, or delete your data, restrict or object to its use, and take it elsewhere (portability). Just email support@spanified.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority — in Spain, the AEPD.

Sharing

We share your email only with the services that run the newsletter for us: our email delivery service and Cloudflare (which processes the subscribe form). If you consent to analytics or marketing cookies, Google and Meta receive the usage data described above; both may process it in the United States under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. We never sell personal data and never share your email with advertisers. If the law requires disclosure, we will comply — and tell you unless forbidden.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will note it here and, for subscribers, in the newsletter. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
