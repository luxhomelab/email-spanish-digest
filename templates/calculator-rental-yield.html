How this calculator models Spanish rental taxes

Purchase: ITP on resales and IVA/IGIC plus AJD on new builds, using the regional rates from our Property Buying Cost Calculator. Rental income: residents pay IRPF on net income after a reduction you choose, non-residents pay IRNR at 19% (EU/EEA, net) or 24% (elsewhere, gross). Sale: residents pay the 2026 savings scale, non-residents a flat 19%. Get Spain property news in English every morning / Compare self-employed taxes with our Autónomo Tax Calculator.

Sources: BOE-A-2026-20823 (RDL 29/2026); art. 23.2 LIRPF; arts. 24.6 and 25 TRLIRNR; art. 14 RIRPF; Audiencia Nacional 28.07.2025 (rec. 636/2021); savings scale 2026 (guiafiscal.es). Last tax data verification: October 2026. This calculator is an educational simulation and not tax or legal advice.
