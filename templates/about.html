About Spanified

Spain's daily news, in English, in five minutes.

What this is

Spanified publishes the Spain Daily Digest — a free morning briefing with the day's most important Spanish news, summarized in English. It lands every day around 08:00 Madrid time, on this site and by email for subscribers. Past editions live in the archive.

Methodology

  1. Scan. Every morning our pipeline reads Spain's leading news outlets — national dailies, wires, and regional press.
  2. Select. The most significant stories of the day are picked across politics, economy, migration, culture, and sport.
  3. Summarize. Each story is condensed into a short English summary with the help of AI — just the facts, no commentary.
  4. Link the sources. Every story carries links to the original reporting, so you can verify anything yourself in one click.
  5. Human review. The editor reads every digest before it goes out — checking stories, summaries, and sources, and cutting anything below the bar.

Our AI policy

Every digest states it openly: the summaries are based on real news and created with the help of AI. We never invent events, quotes, or figures — and the linked sources are always there to check us. If you spot a mistake, tell us and we'll fix it.

Free, no account needed

The site and the archive are free to read. The email edition is free too — subscribe in one step, unsubscribe anytime.
