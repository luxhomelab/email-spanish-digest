What is Spain Daily?

Spain moves fast — rents, taxes, visas, jobs. Every morning we read the Spanish press cover to cover and hand you only the news that affects your life: a daily Spain news digest with short summaries in plain English, zero clickbait.

☕ Live in Spain, planning the move, or just watching from afar — five minutes with coffee and you're up to speed.

Which Spanish newspapers do we read?

Spain Daily is a newsletter, not a newspaper. We do not report the news ourselves: every day we read the Spanish press, translate and summarize the stories in English, and link each one to its source. We read El País, Cinco Días, El Mundo, La Vanguardia, elDiario.es, El Confidencial, 20 Minutos, Heraldo, Diario Sur, Expansión and the Europa Press agency. International news makes the cut only when it directly affects Spain. It is Spanish news in English, built for the English-language reader who wants the Spanish press without reading it in Spanish.
