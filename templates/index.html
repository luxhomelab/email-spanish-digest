What is Spain Daily?

Spain moves fast. Every morning we read the Spanish press cover to cover and hand you only the news that affects your life.

  • What you get A daily Spain news digest: short summaries in plain English, zero clickbait.
  • What it covers Rents, taxes, visas, jobs and the other stories that change daily life here.
  • Who it's for People who live in Spain, are planning the move or are watching from afar.

Which Spanish newspapers do we read?

Spain Daily is a newsletter, not a newspaper. We do not report the news ourselves: every day we aggregate the Spanish press, translate and summarize the stories in English, and link each one to its source.

  • El País
  • El Mundo
  • La Vanguardia
  • Cinco Días
  • Expansión
  • elDiario.es
  • El Confidencial
  • 20 Minutos
  • Heraldo
  • Diario Sur
  • Europa Press agency

International news makes the cut only when it directly affects Spain. It is Spanish news in English, built for the English-language reader who wants the Spanish press without reading it in Spanish.

How it works

  1. We aggregate the Spanish press National dailies, wires and regional titles. We pick what affects your life: rents, taxes, visas, jobs.
  2. We summarize in plain English Short factual briefs with links to the sources, AI-assisted and checked by the editor before every send.
  3. You read it in 5 minutes In your inbox around 08:00 Madrid time, seven days a week.
